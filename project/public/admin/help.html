01 / INVENTORY

A new PC starts here.

  1. Open PC inventory. Choose a PC to edit or create a new entry. The dashboard’s Edit buttons jump straight to a listed PC.
  2. Tell customers about it. Add the real name, description, specifications, price, and photo. Leave unknown specs blank. A blank price displays “Call for pricing”.
  3. Choose its availability. New PCs begin as Draft. Choose Available when the PC is ready for the catalog, then save.
  4. Review the result. Give the preview a minute or two to rebuild, then check the photo, price, specs, and buttons on the website.

Use Pages & contact for your headlines, story, phone, address, and visiting note. Use Repair services for service descriptions, highlights, and button destinations.

02 / AVAILABILITY

The right status, at a glance.

Available

Visible in the catalog and ready for inquiries.

Reserved

Visible with a reserved label. Customers can ask about alternatives.

Draft

Hidden while you prepare the listing.

Sold

Hidden from shoppers. The record stays in your inventory.

After a sale, open that PC, change Availability to Sold, and save. No need to delete it.

03 / LISTING DETAILS

Give each PC a proper introduction.

Choose a clear photo.

Use a JPG, PNG, or WebP up to 10 MB. New uploads are resized for faster loading. Portrait photos display in full, and shoppers can open PC details for a larger look. Add a short photo description, or leave it blank to use the PC name.

Connect the matching Marketplace ad.

Paste the PC’s item link into Facebook Marketplace listing URL. Use the individual ad, not your profile URL. This adds a Marketplace button to the card and its details view.

After checking the ad’s price and stock, enter the date in Marketplace availability last checked using YYYY-MM-DD. When the ad changes, update the PC here too. The link does not automatically sync Facebook changes.

Preview while you edit.

The PC preview shows its photo, price, specs, Marketplace link, and whether the selected status makes it visible. Field limits and save checks help catch mistakes before they reach the preview website.

04 / YOUR REVIEW WEBSITE

Edit. Save. Take a look.

Saved edits update the review branch, codex/cms-preview. Open this website’s catalog to review the result. The editor may call its save action “Publish”; in this setup it saves to the review website.

A live release is a separate step.

Changes go to upgradegamingpcs.com after an owner-approved release. Your CMS review tracks the changes and checks.

Check the affected PC or page on a phone too. If a build fails, the previous successful preview remains available while the content issue is corrected.

05 / OWNER ACCESS

Get in. Get back to your shop.

Owner editing uses a GitHub editing key with access to this website’s repository. The CMS can remember it in your trusted browser.

First sign-in or an expired editing key
  1. Sign into the GitHub account that owns or can write to Wilson0279737/UpGPC_Codex.
  2. Create a fine-grained personal access token. Choose only this repository, a short expiry, and Contents: Read and write.
  3. Open the editor, choose Sign In Using Access Token, and paste the key there. Keep it private. Never paste it into a PC description, page, screenshot, or chat.

The editor remembers the key in browser local storage. Sign out through its account menu when appropriate. Revoke the key in GitHub if the computer is lost or access should end. GitHub handles your account password and recovery.

What about Google sign-in?

Google customer accounts are available on the account test website. An owner with the assigned admin role sees an Open website editor link. Editing still requires the separate GitHub key. Ordinary customers do not receive editing permission.

Keep private customer information out of PC listings and website content.

06 / TRY IT FIRST

A space to get comfortable.

The practice editor needs no account. Try a price, image, status, or headline. Saved practice edits stay in this browser after a reload, separate from the website and GitHub.

To reset your practice copy, use the editor’s Settings → Data Management and erase the practice data. This restores the sample content in this browser.
